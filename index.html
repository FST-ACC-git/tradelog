How to get your E*TRADE CSV
1. Log in to etrade.com on a computer
2. Go to Accounts → History
3. Set your full date range
4. Click Download → choose CSV
5. Email or AirDrop it to your iPhone
6. Open email → tap CSV → Share → TradeLog
